06 / Good to know

Questions, answered

Need to know
something?

A few quick answers before you pull up a chair.

Do you take walk-ins?+

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome, though reservations are recommended for larger groups and busy evenings.

Do you offer vegetarian options?+

Absolutely. Our menu changes with the seasons and always includes plant-forward dishes.

Can I book for a celebration?+

Yes. Tell us the occasion in your booking request and the team will help make it feel special.

Where can I find you?+

We are at 20 Riverside Drive, Nairobi, Kenya. Our kitchen is open Tuesday through Sunday.
